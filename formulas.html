Stock Formulas

The three rules every scanned and entered row must follow.

1. Total stock

Total = Opening + Add
Example: Opening 41 + Add 25 = Total 66

2. Sales

Sales = Total − Closing
Example: Total 66 − Closing 59 = Sales 7

3. Amount

Amount = Sales × Price
Example: Sales 7 × Price 300 = Amount 2100
